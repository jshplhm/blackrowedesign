The Problem

Pretty doesn't
pay the rent.

Most commercial spaces are designed by people who think about aesthetics first. The result looks good on Instagram and fails in practice — customers confused, staff frustrated, conversions flat.

Blackrowe Design flips that. We start with how your space needs to function for your specific business, then build an environment around that logic. The aesthetic is the last layer, not the first.

01
Poor customer flow costs you revenue
Confused customers leave. Friction at the wrong moment kills conversions. Spatial logic is a direct business lever most owners never touch.
02
Generic spaces don't build brands
When your space looks like everyone else's, you're competing on price. A distinctive environment gives customers something to remember — and talk about.
03
Expensive mistakes happen early
A floor plan committed to at signing is almost impossible to fix later. Getting spatial strategy right before build-out is orders of magnitude cheaper than fixing it after.
04
Most designers don't think like business owners
Beautiful portfolios, no ROI conversation. Blackrowe Design starts with your business model and works backwards to the space.

Worked Scenarios

How we
think.

These are worked spatial scenarios — real business types, real constraints, real decisions. They show how Blackrowe Design approaches a space before touching a single finish selection.

Wellness
Studio
1,400 sq ft

New yoga and massage studio, strip mall lease

A long rectangular space with a single entrance. The challenge: create clear separation between retail, active studio, and treatment rooms — without expensive construction — while delivering a calm, premium feel from the moment you walk in.

↳ Spatial concept · floor plan · finish direction

→Entry zone slows arrival and signals transition from street to studio
→Treatment rooms acoustically separated using built-ins, not walls
→Retail placed at natural pause point in customer flow
→Natural light preserved and amplified throughout
Independent
Café
900 sq ft

First-time café owner, former retail space

High foot traffic corner location. Priorities: fast throughput at peak hours, a reason to stay and spend more, and a spatial identity that feels local and considered — not generic.

↳ Spatial concept · floor plan · finish direction

→Order and pickup separated to eliminate queue conflict
→Seating zoned by dwell time — quick solo, lingering social
→Window seating activated as street-visible amenity
→Merchandise placed at natural exit pause point
Professional
Office
2,200 sq ft

Boutique law firm, newly leased floor

A firm moving from shared space into its first dedicated floor. Needs to signal credibility from arrival while giving attorneys the privacy they require — without a closed-corridor feel.

↳ Spatial concept · floor plan · finish direction

→Reception designed as a composed arrival, not a waiting room
→Conference room accessible to clients without exposing working areas
→Offices acoustically separated by material, not distance
→Finishes calibrated to signal expertise without coldness

FAQ

Common
questions.

Everything you need to know before starting a conversation. If something isn't covered here, just call or text — 530-557-7879.

What does Blackrowe Design actually do?
Blackrowe Design is a commercial spatial design consultancy. We help businesses — retail stores, restaurants, wellness studios, professional offices — design spaces that work: guiding customers, supporting staff, and expressing the brand clearly. We work on spatial strategy (how a space is organized and flows), design concept (the look, feel, and finish direction), and implementation support (on-site during build-out).
Where are you located and what areas do you serve?
Blackrowe Design is based in Nevada City, California, in the Sierra Nevada foothills. We primarily serve clients in Sacramento, Reno, and the Lake Tahoe region, and are available for remote projects anywhere in the US.
How is this different from hiring an interior designer?
Most interior designers lead with aesthetics — what the space looks like. Blackrowe Design leads with function — how the space works for your specific business. We think about customer flow, zoning, conversion points, and operational logic first. The aesthetic follows from that foundation, not the other way around.
What's the first step?
A free consultation — no pitch, no commitment. Just a conversation about your space, what you're planning, and what you're trying to accomplish. Call or text 530-557-7879, or use the contact form below.
What types of spaces do you work with?
We work with retail stores, restaurants and cafés, hospitality and wellness studios, professional offices, and other commercial spaces. If you have a commercial lease and a business to run out of it, we can probably help.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. Spatial Strategy and Concept & Direction can both be delivered remotely with strong results. Implementation Support requires on-site presence, which we offer within the Sacramento–Reno–Tahoe region.
